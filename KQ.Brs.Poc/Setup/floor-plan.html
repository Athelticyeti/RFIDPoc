KQ-504 baggage demo · boardroom setup

Floor plan for the setup team. One Alien ALR-9900+ reader, four antennas, three airport areas. A bag travels left to right: check-in (take-on) → sorting tunnel → loading at Hold 2.
Room ≈ 15 m × 6 m · scale 1 square = 1 m
Reader 192.168.0.161 · laptop 192.168.0.3

Where

Mount

Props

Bag Sorted + BPM sent. A KQ-412 bag sets off the alarm here.

Setup checklist

  1. T−60Tape floor marks at 2.5 m, 7.5 m, 12.5 m. Place the three stations and the operator table against the wall at the centre.
  2. T−50Plug antennas into reader ports 0, 1, 2, 3 exactly as above. Tape cables down; cable cover where port 2 crosses the walkway.
  3. T−45Power the reader, network cable to the laptop, HDMI to the screen. Close RFIDPoc.
  4. T−40Start KQ.Brs.Poc. Allow it through Windows Firewall if asked. Reader pill turns green: "Reader streaming".
  5. T−35Calibrate on Live reads: all tags in the tin → all four antennas at 0 reads/s. Walk one tag desk → tunnel → ramp; only that area's antenna should read.
  6. T−25Cross-reads? Settings → Antenna → Min RSSI halfway between the weak and strong reading (e.g. 1,500 and 9,000 → 4,000). Save, walk again.
  7. T−10Settings → Reset demo (empty flight, bindings unticked). Dashboard → Add passenger for each guest. Loading page: ULD AK8, loading antenna not armed.
  8. T−0Screen on the Dashboard. Spare tags in the closed tin.

Kit

  • ALR-9900+ reader and power supply
  • 4 antennas + 4 cables (check ≥ 6 m)
  • Network cable reader ↔ laptop
  • Laptop with KQ.Brs.Poc, HDMI to screen
  • 2 small tables or chairs (desk, ramp)
  • 2 chairs or stands (tunnel)
  • 2 boxes labelled AK7 and AK8
  • Metal tin with lid (tag store)
  • 3–5 tagged props, luggage labels, marker
  • Gaffer tape and a cable cover

Assumptions

Room about 15 × 6 m with the screen at one end. Reader and laptop sit at the centre so every antenna is within 5 m of the reader.

Cables shorter than 6 m? Move the desk and ramp inwards to the cable limit and keep the tunnel centred.

Reader power stays at full (RFAttenuation 0) unless Min RSSI can't stop cross-reads.

Two people: a presenter carries the bags; an operator drives the laptop.
